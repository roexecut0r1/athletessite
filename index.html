Where Athletes and Real-World Answers Come Together

Answers Through Athletes’ mission is to provide athletes, especially those who have faced adversity, injury, or illness, a place to stay connected and relevant to the sports and the community they love. Through volunteerism and fundraising ATA’s goal is able to give back to the community that has supported them and to support others who are in need. ATA is more than a non-profit. ATA works to turn the unexpected hard circumstances into a movement of service and connection.
